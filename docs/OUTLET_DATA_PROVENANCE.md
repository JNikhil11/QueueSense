> All campus dining listings are categorized as `STUDENT_SOURCED` or `COMMUNITY_LISTING`, derived from publicly circulated student orientation materials, mentorship publications, and crowdsourced campus feedback.

---

## 1. Provenance Policy & Verification Standards

To uphold scientific and operational integrity, QueueSense classifies listings under strict provenance tiers:

| Status Code | Meaning | Verification Standard |
|---|---|---|
| `STUDENT_SOURCED` | Authentic physical dining location compiled from peer student mentor guides and student council publications. | Sourced from public student orientation guides (e.g. Student Mentor Program / Gymkhana guides) and ambassador inspection. |
| `COMMUNITY_LISTING` | Location contributed by campus student peers via crowdsourced suggestion. | Under verification; requires confirmation by at least 3 distinct student reports before permanent listing. |
| `OPERATOR_VERIFIED` | Formally confirmed by the physical mess contractor or dining hall management. | Reserved strictly for outlets where the operating caterer has validated operating hours and capacity. (Currently 0 outlets carry this tier). |

## 4. Operator Corrections & Claim Policy

Dining caterers, mess committee student representatives, or campus administrative bodies may request corrections, adjust operating hours, or claim their outlet listing:

1. Submit an issue or pull request on GitHub at [`https://github.com/JNikhil11/QueueSense`](https://github.com/JNikhil11/QueueSense).
2. Reference the exact `Outlet ID` and attach verification evidence (e.g. mess menu schedule, council notification, or operating timetable).
3. The register is reviewed and maintained under version control in this repository.
